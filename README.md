An entry for the make.fun micro-competition **Two Lines to Your Past Self**.

Line 1 warns your past self about one specific moment; line 2 says why
that moment mattered and what changed because of it. The moment here is
an extra shift taken on the night a daughter takes her first steps — a
single missed evening that turns into ten years of arriving too late.

## Entry

> Do not take the extra shift on the night your daughter takes her first steps across the kitchen floor.
>
> You missed the only first step she ever took, and you spent the next ten years trying to be in rooms you had already left.

(Line 1: 102 characters; line 2: 122 characters; limit 200 per line.)

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

`main.py` is stdlib-only and resolves `entry.txt` relative to its own
location, so it runs from any working directory.
